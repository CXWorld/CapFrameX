## Generic hardware starter profiles

The starter profiles for whole hardware families name no CPU model in their
graphics. They embed PNGs rendered from the vectors above at 717 x 717 pixels:
FUSION ARL `Intel/Intel-Core-Ultra.svg` (its full viewBox), FUSION RPL
`Intel/Intel-Core.svg` with a transparent margin of 7.5% on each edge, so that
its badge has the size of the Core Ultra badge. REDLINE and FUSION Ryzen show the
Ryzen badge template described below without its model and tier layers.

## Ryzen 5 9600X badge components

The files in `AMD/Ryzen-Badge/` are CapFrameX vector adaptations of the
Ryzen 5 9600X badge image supplied by the user on 2026-10-12. They are not
unmodified manufacturer downloads. The metallic housing, orange tracks,
diagonal hatching, inset panel and model/tier lettering were drawn
as vector geometry from that reference. The orange and silver tones were
matched visually to the reference; they do not claim to define AMD brand colors.

The AMD corporate artwork reuses the original path/polygon geometry from
`AMD/AMD.svg`, sourced from AMD above. The Ryzen wordmark reuses the first six
subpaths (the Ryzen lettering, before the AMD portion) of original `path847`
from `AMD/AMD-Ryzen-Transparent.svg`. Their only adaptations are placement,
uniform scaling and silver/black fills. No logo lettering was redrawn.

All badge files are genuine SVGs without raster images, external resources,
font references, text elements or SVG filters. Gradients and individually
drawn diagonal lines provide the surface details. Rounded corners retain
transparency outside the badge.

The horizontal highlight above the Ryzen wordmark was removed from the housing,
template and both complete badges on 2026-10-12. The embedded palette and profile
PNGs are regenerated from the same corrected SVG geometry.

container, use the following frames and stretch each image to its frame. Scale

| Template | 0 | 0 | 320 | 320 |
| Model | 260 | 86 | 40 | 155 |

The editor's **Ryzen badge** palette item creates this three-layer container.
Its portable PNG resources are rendered from the template, model and tier SVGs
at twice their tile size: 640 x 640, 80 x 310 and 90 x 80 pixels, respectively.
The vector originals remain available here for editing or importing individual
components. The model and tier are replaceable image layers, not live text fields.

The Ryzen 7 9800X3D variant uses the same three layer frames. Its longer model
lettering is uniformly reduced and horizontally centered within the 40 x 155
model canvas. The 9, 0 and X shapes are reused from the reference-style model;
8, 3, D and tier 7 are new vector lettering in that same style. This is a
CapFrameX customization, not a claim to reproduce an official 9800X3D badge.
The original Ryzen 5 / 9600X SVG files remain unchanged.

The inset silver panel occupies approximately `(93, 101, 136, 125)`. The top
Ryzen wordmark starts at `(73, 26)` with width 174. The inset AMD wordmark starts
at `(99, 132)` with width 121; its Ryzen wordmark starts at `(98, 168)` with width
124. The lower AMD wordmark starts at `(25, 265)` with width 119.

## Anime starter profiles

CHERRY BLOSSOM, NEON NIGHT and LUNAR (`overlay-templates/Anime-*.json`) embed
original artwork made for CapFrameX on 2026-10-24. It contains no third-party
material: no existing character, logo, brand mark, photograph or font glyph.
`scripts/New-AnimeOverlayArt.py` draws every picture from SVG primitives and
renders it through headless Microsoft Edge; its fixed random seeds reproduce
the embedded PNGs byte for byte. The SVGs use filters and masks, so they are
not offered here as importable graphics.

Each design embeds two PNGs, exported at 1.6 pixels per design unit: a
608 x 256 banner behind the channel name and a 266 x 288 character on a
transparent background whose head rises above the banner. The characters
avoid trait combinations that identify well-known characters (for example
pink hair with fox ears and violet eyes, or teal hair with a headset). The
background patterns are traditional public-domain motifs: seigaiha waves
(CHERRY BLOSSOM), hui wen fret and auspicious clouds (LUNAR). Neon signs and
lanterns carry no lettering. All text in the designs, such as the channel
name, stays in editable text tiles.

## CapFrameX logo tiles

The editor's **CapFrameX banner** and **CapFrameX logo** palette items embed two
PNGs of CapFrameX's own mark; no third-party material. They are not files of
this folder: `source/CapFrameX/Icons/New-OverlayLogoTiles.ps1` renders them
from the production masters `Icons/CX_Concepts/Production/CX_A1_Mark_Master.png`
and `CX_A1_Wordmark_Master.png` into the OSD Controls (`Resources/Logos`).
Both are transparent and carry only the white mark and wordmark with their lime
X; the tile behind them supplies the brand blue `#0271F9`. The banner
(1280 x 256, 5:1) places mark and wordmark side by side in the proportions of
`Icons/CX_Installer_Banner.png`, with the same margin on all four sides. The
logo (512 x 512) places the mark where the app icon does.
